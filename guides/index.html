Match the hardware

Read the printed model and board revision. A matching ASIC name alone does not make another board’s firmware compatible.

Keep a private settings record

Preservation is optional and is not a backup. Record what you need to reconnect and restore your mining configuration.

Verify after the reset

A completed write is one checkpoint. Confirm boot, network access, cooling, and accepted work on the miner and mining service.
